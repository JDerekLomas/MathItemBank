# Comprehensive AI Skills Architecture - 100 Skills Framework

## 🎯 Skill Categories (100 Skills Total)

### 1. Content Generation & Creation (20 Skills)
- Core item generation and content development workflows

### 2. Evaluation & Quality Assurance (25 Skills)
- Comprehensive assessment quality evaluation and validation

### 3. Accessibility & Equity (15 Skills)
- Universal design, cultural responsiveness, and bias prevention

### 4. Analytics & Insights (20 Skills)
- Data analysis, psychometrics, and performance optimization

### 5. Integration & Workflow (10 Skills)
- System integration, automation, and process management

### 6. Specialized Domains (10 Skills)
- Subject-specific and contextually-focused skills
