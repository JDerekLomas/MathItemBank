## Sample Item 1: Linear Equations (Grade 9)

### Item Content
**Standard:** CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSA.CED.A.1 - Create equations and inequalities in one variable and use them to solve problems.

**Item Type:** Multiple Choice
**Difficulty:** Proficient
**DOK Level:** 2

**Question:**
A cell phone plan costs $30 per month plus $0.15 per gigabyte of data used. If a customer's total bill for one month was $48, how many gigabytes of data did the customer use?

**Options:**
A) 120 GB
B) 18 GB
C) 12 GB
D) 8 GB

**Correct Answer:** A) 120 GB

**Solution:**
Let g = gigabytes of data used
Total cost = 30 + 0.15g = 48
0.15g = 18
g = 18 ÷ 0.15 = 120

**Explanation:**
The customer used 120 gigabytes of data. We set up the equation 30 + 0.15g = 48 to represent the total monthly cost, then solved for g.

## Sample Item 2: Probability (Grade 9)

### Item Content
**Standard:** CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSS.MD.B.5 - Use expected values to evaluate and compare strategies.

**Item Type:** Multiple Choice
**Difficulty:** Developing
**DOK Level:** 3

**Question:**
A game costs $2 to play. You draw a card from a standard deck. If you draw a heart, you win $5. If you draw a diamond, you win $2. If you draw a club or spade, you win nothing. What is the expected value of playing this game?

**Options:**
A) -$0.25
B) $0.00
C) $0.50
D) $1.25

**Correct Answer:** A) -$0.25

**Solution:**
P(heart) = 13/52 = 1/4, winnings = $5
P(diamond) = 13/52 = 1/4, winnings = $2
P(club or spade) = 26/52 = 1/2, winnings = $0

Expected value = (1/4)($5) + (1/4)($2) + (1/2)($0) - $2
= $1.25 + $0.50 + $0 - $2 = -$0.25

**Explanation:**
The expected value is -$0.25, meaning on average you lose 25 cents per game.

## Sample Item 3: Geometry - Circle Arc Length (Grade 11)

### Item Content
**Standard:** CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSG.C.B.5 - Find arc lengths and areas of sectors of circles.

**Item Type:** Short Answer
**Difficulty:** Proficient
**DOK Level:** 2

**Question:**
A circle has a radius of 8 inches. What is the length of an arc that subtends a central angle of 45°? Express your answer in terms of π.

**Answer:** 2π inches

**Solution:**
Arc length = (central angle/360°) × circumference
Arc length = (45/360) × 2π(8)
Arc length = (1/8) × 16π = 2π inches

**Explanation:**
The arc length is found by taking the fraction of the circle represented by the central angle (45°/360° = 1/8) and multiplying by the full circumference (2πr = 16π inches).

## Sample Item 4: Quadratic Functions (Grade 10)

### Item Content
**Standard:** CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSF.IF.B.4 - Interpret key features of graphs and tables in terms of quantities.

**Item Type:** Multiple Choice
**Difficulty:** Advanced
**DOK Level:** 3

**Question:**
The function f(x) = -x² + 6x - 8 models the height (in feet) of a ball thrown into the air as a function of time (in seconds). What is the maximum height the ball reaches?

**Options:**
A) 1 foot
B) 3 feet
C) 8 feet
D) 9 feet

**Correct Answer:** A) 1 foot

**Solution:**
The maximum occurs at the vertex. The x-coordinate of the vertex is:
x = -b/(2a) = -6/(2(-1)) = 3

The y-coordinate (maximum height) is:
f(3) = -(3)² + 6(3) - 8 = -9 + 18 - 8 = 1 foot

**Explanation:**
The maximum height of 1 foot occurs at 3 seconds. Note: This seems like an unusually low maximum height, which might indicate a problem with the function parameters.

## Sample Item 5: Statistics - Residuals (Grade 11)

### Item Content
**Standard:** CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSS.ID.B.6 - Informally assess the fit of a function by plotting and analyzing residuals.

**Item Type:** Extended Response
**Difficulty:** Advanced
**DOK Level:** 4

**Question:**
A linear model predicts test scores based on study hours. The residual plot shows the following pattern:
- For 0-2 study hours: residuals are mostly positive
- For 3-5 study hours: residuals are randomly scattered around zero
- For 6-8 study hours: residuals are mostly negative

Based on this residual pattern, evaluate whether a linear model is appropriate for this data. Explain your reasoning and suggest a better model if needed.

**Sample Response:**
A linear model is not appropriate for this data. The residual pattern shows a systematic curve - positive residuals at low study hours, random scatter in the middle, and negative residuals at high study hours. This suggests the relationship between study hours and test scores is not linear but rather follows a curve where gains diminish with more study time.

A better model would be a logarithmic or square root function that captures the diminishing returns pattern. These models typically show rapid improvement initially, then level off, which matches the residual pattern we observe.

**Scoring Rubric:**
- 4 points: Correctly identifies non-appropriateness, explains the systematic pattern, and suggests appropriate alternative model
- 3 points: Correctly identifies non-appropriateness and explains pattern but doesn't suggest appropriate alternative
- 2 points: Identifies non-appropriateness but explanation is unclear
- 1 point: Minimal or incorrect analysis
